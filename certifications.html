I'm an organizer for Storyboard, a Hack Club YSWS (You Ship [a technical project], We Ship [a prize]) where teenagers can create a Visual Novel video game and earn prizes like drawing tablets and Steam licenses!
I host workrooms and provide support for coding using Ren'Py through the Hack Club Slack. I also created a slideshow to easily teach Ren'Py to teens who have never coded before, and it was shown in over 100 Daydream events around the world!
